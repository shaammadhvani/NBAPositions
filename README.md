Currently, basketball has 5 positions: point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, and center. As time has passed, players within each position group
no longer completely reflect the traditional roles and expectations of said position; yet, they are still classified into that position due to attribute such as
height, weight, and mentality, among other non-quantifiable traits. Instead, is it possible to re-classify players solely based on performance and statistics?
This reclassificatiion process could help teams within the NBA better build championship caliber teams, a la the Golden State Warriors from 2015 to now, who seldom
use traditional centers yet have found consistent succcess.
